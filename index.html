    Data & approach

    How it works

    Prices are estimates from historical valuations and statistical models. They help compare areas; they are not a substitute for a current valuation.

    Data and modelling

    Property data, including 2005 valuations, are from Land & Property Services. Values are updated using NISRA's Northern Ireland House Price Index, with separate factors for houses and flats/apartments and adjustments at local government district level.

    Overall price levels reflect the latest data, while small-area patterns are based on 2005 valuations and may not capture subsequent changes in an area's relative desirability.

    Postcode values come from a statistical model of price per square metre based on property type, floor area, garden, garage, and postcode. Only postcodes with at least 10 properties are shown, and spatial smoothing reduces noise by sharing information between nearby postcodes.

    The map colours show postcode coefficients relative to the overall NI average. These capture area value separately from the particular mix of property sizes and features in each postcode.

    The affordability calculator uses simpler house and flat models. New builds are not separately modeled, so the estimate is increased by a factor of 1.4. For a short postcode, the 70th percentile is used for new-build estimates because new developments may be in more desirable parts of the area.

    Processing and modelling code is available on GitHub.

    Model validation

    A sample of sales listings from PropertyNews (October 2025) was used to assess the calculator. Floor area and garden/garage details were linked to LPS data, which may be outdated for some homes.

    Predictions were reasonably well calibrated. Mean absolute error was £34,000 and mean absolute percentage error was 14%. Actual homes can still be more or less expensive depending on features not included in this model.

    The goal is a useful estimate of a typical property—not the most accurate possible price prediction for an individual home.
